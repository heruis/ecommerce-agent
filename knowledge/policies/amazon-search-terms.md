## Length limit

Backend search terms are limited to under 250 bytes. Note that this is bytes, not characters. Terms beyond the limit are ignored.

## What to include

Use synonyms, spelling variations and abbreviations a shopper might type that are not already in the title or bullets. Separate terms with spaces; punctuation is not needed. Do not repeat words.

## What to leave out

Do not include brand names (yours or competitors'), ASINs, temporary statements such as "new" or "on sale", subjective claims such as "best", "cheapest" or "amazing", or offensive terms.
