## Bullet points

Use bullet points to state concrete features and benefits: material, size, capacity, what is in the box, how it is used. Most categories show up to five bullets. Keep each one focused on a single idea.

## Content that is not allowed

Listing copy must not contain pricing or promotional information, shipping details, seller contact information (email, phone, social handles), links to websites, or references to other sellers' products.

## Accuracy of the detail page

Everything on the detail page must describe the product accurately. Do not add claims about certifications, awards or rankings that the seller cannot document.

## Formatting

Avoid HTML, emojis and ALL-CAPS text. Write in the language of the marketplace; untranslated text makes the listing hard to search and may be suppressed.
