## General environmental benefit claims

Unqualified claims such as "eco-friendly", "green" or "environmentally friendly" are hard to substantiate because they imply broad benefits. Marketers should qualify them with a specific, provable attribute, for example "made with 30% recycled plastic".

## Degradable and compostable claims

An unqualified "biodegradable" claim implies the whole item will break down and return to nature within a reasonably short time after customary disposal. Items that usually go to landfill generally cannot make that claim. "Compostable" claims need competent evidence, such as certification to a recognized standard.

## Recyclable claims

A product should only be called recyclable if collection facilities are available to a substantial majority of consumers where it is sold; otherwise the claim must be qualified.
