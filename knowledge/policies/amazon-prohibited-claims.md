## Disease and treatment claims

Ordinary consumer products must not claim to cure, treat, mitigate or prevent a disease. Words such as "cures", "treats", "heals", "anti-inflammatory", "relieves pain" or "detoxifies" can cause the product to be treated as an unapproved drug or medical device, and the listing can be removed.

## Absolute safety promises

Statements like "100% safe", "completely harmless" or "zero risk" are absolute promises that cannot be substantiated. Replace them with specific, documented facts, such as the material grade or the standard a test report shows the product meets.

## When a claim is allowed

A specific claim is acceptable when the seller can document it, for example a third-party lab report showing the material is BPA-free. Keep the wording as narrow as the evidence.
