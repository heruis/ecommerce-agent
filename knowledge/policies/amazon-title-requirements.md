## Title length

Amazon limits product titles to 200 characters including spaces for most categories; some categories set a lower limit. Long titles are truncated on mobile, so put the product type and the most important attributes first. When a title is too long, rewrite it rather than cutting it off, so no key attribute is lost.

## Special characters in titles

Titles must not contain decorative special characters such as ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ unless they are part of the registered brand name.

## Repeated words

The same word should not appear more than twice in a title. Prepositions, articles and conjunctions are excluded from this rule.

## Promotional and subjective language

Titles must not include promotional or subjective phrases such as "free shipping", "100% quality guaranteed", "hot item", "best seller" or "#1". Sales rank, pricing and shipping information do not belong in the title.
