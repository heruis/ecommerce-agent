## Objective claims need a reasonable basis

Under the FTC Act, advertising must be truthful and not misleading, and an advertiser must have a reasonable basis for objective claims before making them. Claims about safety, performance, materials or test results require competent and reliable evidence.

## Express and implied claims

Both what a listing says directly and what it reasonably implies count. "Non-toxic" implies the product poses no toxicity risk under normal use; it needs evidence, and a narrower, tested statement is safer.

## Puffery

Vague, subjective praise that no reasonable shopper would read as a measurable fact ("delightful design") is generally treated as puffery. Specific, measurable statements ("lasts twice as long") are objective claims and need proof.
