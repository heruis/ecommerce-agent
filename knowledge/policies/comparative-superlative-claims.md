## Sales-rank claims

"#1 best seller", "number one in America" or "top selling" are objective claims about market position. They need current, verifiable data covering the market and time period stated, and Amazon does not allow best-seller language in titles. Remove them from listing copy.

## Superlatives

Words such as "best" or "world's finest" are usually subjective. They add little information and can read as an unsubstantiated comparison. Replace them with a concrete benefit the shopper can check, for example "folds to 1.5 cm for pockets and backpacks".

## Comparisons with other brands

Do not name competitor brands or claim superiority over them without documented, like-for-like test results.
