## Germ-killing claims are pesticide claims

In the US, a product that claims to kill, repel or control bacteria, viruses, mold or other microbes is making a pesticide claim. Pesticides generally require EPA registration.

## Treated articles

Some products are treated with a registered antimicrobial only to protect the product itself (for example, to prevent odor in the product). Such treated articles may only make claims about protecting the product, not about protecting the user or killing germs on contact.

## Marketplace enforcement

Major marketplaces remove listings that make unregistered pesticide claims such as "antibacterial cutting board" or "kills 99.9% of germs". Remove these claims unless the seller provides an EPA registration number for the product.
