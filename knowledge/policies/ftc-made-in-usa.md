## All or virtually all

An unqualified "Made in USA" claim means all or virtually all of the product, including its significant parts and processing, is of US origin. A product manufactured abroad and imported cannot carry the claim.

## Qualified claims

A qualified claim such as "Assembled in USA from imported parts" must be accurate. For products made in China, describe the product without origin claims, or state the true country of origin when the category requires it.
